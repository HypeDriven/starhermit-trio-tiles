      How to play

      The idea Tap an uncovered tile to lift it into your tray. Three identical symbols in the tray clear themselves. Empty the whole table to win.
      The tray The tray holds seven tiles. If every slot is filled with no triple, the round is lost. Keep sets moving.
      Covered tiles A tile under another is shadowed and cannot be picked. Clear the tiles above to free it.
      Combos Clear triples within a few turns of each other to chain a combo bonus.
      Controls Tap or click a tile. Arrow keys move focus between available tiles, Enter picks, U undoes, H hints, C resets the camera, Esc pauses.
      Fair play Every table is seeded and proven solvable. Ranked scores are verified by replaying your moves on the server.
